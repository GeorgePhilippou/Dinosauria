Dinosauria v2 — an open scientific reference to 328 non-avian dinosaur genera. Species facts from the NHM London Dinosaur Directory; occurrence, stratigraphic and taxonomy enrichment from the Paleobiology Database; geological ages from the IUGS International Chronostratigraphic Chart; images from Wikimedia Commons (individual CC licences). Editorial corrections and gap-fills are documented in CHANGELOG-v2.md. Built on the Dinosauria dataset by George Philippou · CC BY-NC 4.0.

## Overview

Alectrosaurus was a mid-sized tyrannosauroid from Late Cretaceous Asia. PBDB records a broad Central Asian distribution including Inner Mongolia, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan and Tajikistan, with formations such as Iren Dabasu, Bissekty, Khodzhakul and Bostobe.

Its importance lies in the pre-Tyrannosaurus phase of tyrannosauroid history: Alectrosaurus documents a more gracile, geographically widespread Asian predator before the deepest-skulled tyrannosaurids dominated latest Cretaceous ecosystems.

## Anatomy & life

Alectrosaurus helps bridge the gap between early tyrannosauroids and the later giant tyrannosaurids of Asia and North America.

The PBDB occurrence spread is broad, so some referred material may need taxonomic review rather than being treated as equally secure.

## Fossil record

An associated partial hind limb and limited referred remains.

- The secure type material is chiefly a partial hind limb and foot.
- Recent work supports tyrannosauroid affinities but cannot recover a complete body from the limited diagnostic material.

## Where it lived

The Iren Dabasu Formation has a debated, broad Late Cretaceous age, probably within the Cenomanian-Campanian interval.

## Open questions

- Formation age is poorly constrained.
- Historical cranial and forelimb referrals are excluded.
- Its exact tyrannosauroid position is unresolved.

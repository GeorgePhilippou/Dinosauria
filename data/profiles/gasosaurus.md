## Overview

Gasosaurus (meaning 'gas lizard') was a medium-sized carnivorous theropod from the Middle Jurassic of Sichuan Province, China, living approximately 170–161 million years ago during the Bajocian to Oxfordian stages. Estimated at around 4 metres in length, it is placed within Tetanurae and was described from the Lower Shaximiao Formation — the Dashanpu fossil quarry — one of the richest Middle Jurassic dinosaur sites in the world. Its name pays tribute to the gasoline company whose workers discovered the Dashanpu quarry, now one of China's most significant palaeontological sites.

Gasosaurus constructus was described by Dong Zhiming and Tang Zilu in 1985. As a Mid-Jurassic tetanuran, Gasosaurus represents an important but poorly known predator from a time period and region that remains underrepresented in the theropod fossil record. It shared its ecosystem with large sauropods such as Shunosaurus, Omeisaurus, and Datousaurus, as well as the stegosaur Huayangosaurus.

## Anatomy & life

Gasosaurus is one of very few theropods known from the Middle Jurassic of Asia, a time period poorly documented globally. Its placement within Tetanurae is secure based on available material, but its exact position — whether closer to megalosaurids, allosaurids, or another group — remains uncertain due to the fragmentary nature of the specimen. Better material would significantly clarify its evolutionary relationships.

The Dashanpu quarry is now the site of the Zigong Dinosaur Museum, one of the most important in-situ dinosaur museums in the world, where visitors can observe fossils in their original excavation context. Gasosaurus and its contemporaries from the site have contributed substantially to understanding of Middle Jurassic dinosaur communities in Asia.

## Fossil record

Gasosaurus constructus was described by Dong Zhiming and Tang Zilu in 1985 from the Dashanpu quarry in Zigong, Sichuan. The genus name honours the Chinese gas industry, whose workers discovered the Dashanpu fossil site during construction activities. The species name constructus likewise references the construction work that led to the discovery. The holotype includes partial limb and vertebral material.

One incomplete, headless and disarticulated postcranial skeleton.

- No diagnostic skull is known.
- The principal material includes vertebrae, pelvis and limb bones rather than a complete skeleton.
- Carnivory is likely from theropod identity, but detailed feeding claims are unsupported.

## Where it lived

The Lower Shaximiao Formation at Dashanpu preserves a remarkable Middle Jurassic fauna of sauropods, stegosaurs, and small ornithopods, with Gasosaurus as the principal large predator. It shared this environment with the long-necked sauropods Shunosaurus and Omeisaurus, the stegosaur Huayangosaurus, and the small ornithopod Xiaosaurus. The diversity of herbivores suggests an unusually productive Jurassic ecosystem in what is now Sichuan.

The Dashanpu quarry near Zigong is one of the most important Jurassic dinosaur sites in the world; Gasosaurus is the main theropod known from this formation.

The Lower Shaximiao Formation is generally Bathonian-Callovian; specimen-level numerical dating is unavailable.

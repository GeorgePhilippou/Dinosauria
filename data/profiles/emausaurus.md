## Overview

Emausaurus was a small early armoured dinosaur from northern Germany, known from Toarcian rocks of the Early Jurassic. It sits close to the base of Thyreophora, the lineage that later produced stegosaurs and ankylosaurs, so even fragmentary material is useful for understanding how dinosaur body armour first evolved.

The fossil record is limited, but the animal is important because it is the only formally named dinosaur from its local Toarcian zone and one of the few early Jurassic thyreophorans from Europe. PBDB records it from Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, while Wikipedia notes the Lehmhagen Member as the source unit.

## Anatomy & life

Emausaurus helps fill the early history of armoured dinosaurs before the spectacular plates of stegosaurs and heavy ankylosaur armour evolved.

The genus is based on sparse material, so body size, detailed armour arrangement and ecology should be treated cautiously.

## Fossil record

One incomplete juvenile or subadult partial skeleton with skull fragments and osteoderms.

## Open questions

- Only one immature and incomplete individual is known.
- A modern redescription is needed for armour, anatomy and exact thyreophoran position.
- Specialist confirmation is still required before this profile is marked primary-literature reviewed.

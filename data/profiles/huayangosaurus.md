## Overview

Huayangosaurus (meaning 'Huayang lizard', Huayang being an alternate name for Sichuan province) was a herbivorous stegosaurian dinosaur from the Middle Jurassic of China, living approximately 170–160 million years ago. Found in the Lower Shaximiao Formation of Sichuan Province, it reached about 4 to 4.5 metres in length — much smaller than its famous later relative Stegosaurus. It bore a combination of plates and spines along its back and sides, and is placed in its own family, Huayangosauridae. It was described by Dong, Tang and Zhou in 1982.

Huayangosaurus is one of the most primitive stegosaurs known and pre-dates Stegosaurus by approximately 20 million years. Its anatomy retains several features considered primitive for Stegosauria, including teeth in the premaxilla (the front of the upper jaw) and relatively shorter forelimbs compared to hindlimbs. These primitive characteristics make Huayangosaurus crucial for understanding the early evolution of stegosaurs. It shared the Sichuan landscape with sauropods such as Shunosaurus and Datousaurus and the predator Gasosaurus.

## Anatomy & life

Huayangosaurus retains premaxillary teeth, a feature lost in all later stegosaurs. Its forelimbs are relatively longer and more robust compared to the more gracile forelimbs of derived stegosaurs. The arrangement of plates and spines differs from Stegosaurus, with both spines and plates present rather than predominantly plates. These primitive features make it the most basal well-known stegosaur.

As the most primitive well-preserved stegosaur, Huayangosaurus is a critical reference point for understanding the evolutionary history of Stegosauria. Its Middle Jurassic age and Chinese provenance support Asia as the geographic origin of stegosaurs. The Wikipedia entry notes it predates Stegosaurus by some 20 million years, providing important temporal and morphological context for the group's evolution.

## Fossil record

Huayangosaurus taibaii was described in 1982 by Dong Zhiming, Tang Zilu and Zhou Shiwu from multiple specimens collected from the Zigong area of Sichuan. Named after the ancient name for Sichuan (Huayang) and the poet Li Bai (known as Taibai), it represents one of the best-documented early stegosaurs. The Zigong Dinosaur Museum, where much of the material is housed, has made this fauna world famous.

Multiple partial skeletons with skulls, postcrania and dermal armour.

- Cranial anatomy is unusually well represented for a stegosaur.
- Shoulder spines and dorsal plates are known, while their exact sequence and orientation combine evidence from different specimens.
- It retained several primitive features relative to later stegosaurids.

## Where it lived

The Lower Shaximiao Formation records a warm, humid Middle Jurassic landscape of lakes and rivers in what is now Sichuan. Huayangosaurus lived alongside sauropods including Shunosaurus and Datousaurus, the small ornithopod Xiaosaurus, and the predatory theropod Gasosaurus. This fauna represents one of the best-documented Middle Jurassic dinosaur assemblages globally.

The primary source of Huayangosaurus material, a Bajocian-to-Callovian lacustrine and fluvial deposit that has produced diverse Middle Jurassic dinosaurs.

Lower Shaximiao strata are generally Bathonian-Callovian; published numerical correlations still vary.

## Open questions

- The hypodigm combines disarticulated and incomplete individuals.
- The complete armour arrangement and exact formation age remain uncertain.

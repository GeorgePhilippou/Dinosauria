## Overview

Nedoceratops is one of the most controversial ceratopsid names in the wiki. It is based on a single skull from the Lance Formation of Wyoming and has been interpreted either as a distinct chasmosaurine genus or as an unusual individual of Triceratops.

That uncertainty is the point of the profile: Nedoceratops is less a well-sampled animal than a case study in how palaeontologists decide whether differences in horns, frills and skull openings represent species, growth stage, injury or individual variation.

## Anatomy & life

The genus sits at the centre of debates over Triceratops variation and whether some named ceratopsids are distinct taxa or unusual specimens.

## Fossil record

Single nearly complete skull; no secure postcranium.

## Classification

Treat Nedoceratops as provisionally distinct in the catalogue, but read its profile as a taxonomic problem rather than a stable, well-sampled dinosaur.

## Open questions

- Its generic validity remains actively disputed.
- The only skull may include individual, pathological or ontogenetic variation.
- Specialist confirmation is still required before this profile is marked primary-literature reviewed.

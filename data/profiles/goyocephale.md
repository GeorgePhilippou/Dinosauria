## Overview

Goyocephale was a small pachycephalosaurian ornithischian from Late Cretaceous Mongolia. Unlike many dome-headed dinosaurs known mainly from skull roofs, it is represented by a disarticulated skeleton including much of the skull, limbs, pelvis and vertebrae, giving a broader view of pachycephalosaur anatomy.

Its name means "decorated head", combining Mongolian and Greek roots, and the species name honours Owen Lattimore. PBDB records it from Omnogov, Mongolia, but formation-level data still needs manual confirmation in this dataset.

## Anatomy & life

Goyocephale gives useful postcranial information for pachycephalosaurs, a group often represented by skull domes rather than fuller skeletons.

The local PBDB record lacks formation detail, so ecological comparisons should stay regional until the source locality is manually verified.

## Fossil record

One associated partial skull and incomplete postcranial skeleton.

## Open questions

- Only one incomplete principal individual is known.
- Maturity, diet and head-strike behaviour remain uncertain.
